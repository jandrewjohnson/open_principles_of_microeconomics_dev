The Sales Price of New Single-Family Homes

House prices climbed steadily for thirty years, spiked into 2007, fell back after the crisis and then rose again to new highs

A. New Home Prices Since 1975
Explanation

A house is the largest financial asset most households ever own, and this chart is why the chapter treats it as an investment as well as a place to live. Prices rose steadily from the mid-1970s, accelerated sharply from about 2000, peaked in 2007, and fell back over the next few years as the financial crisis unwound the housing boom.

By the mid-2010s prices had passed their pre-crisis peak, and they rose steeply again at the start of the 2020s. A national figure like this one hides enormous local variation: the same years look very different in different cities. The slider reads the series at a chosen year; the values are sampled from the book's figure.
