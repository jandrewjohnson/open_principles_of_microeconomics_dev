Factors That Shift Demand

The non-price determinants that move the whole curve, outward on the left and inward on the right

A. Increases and Decreases in Demand
Explanation

Panel (a) collects the changes that increase demand, shifting the whole curve out to D₁: a taste shift toward the good, a larger buying population, higher income for a normal good, a rise in the price of substitutes, a fall in the price of complements, and expectations of future scarcity or higher prices.

Panel (b) collects the mirror-image changes that decrease demand, shifting the curve in to D₁. In both panels the price of the good itself is held constant. A change in that price would move the economy along a single curve rather than shifting it.
